# Reflex Delivery MVP — UX Review

## Experience Lead

**Name:** Grace Murigi  
**Role:** Experience Lead

## User Experience Review

The Reflex MVP was tested from the perspective of the three main user roles:

* Retailer
* Dispatcher
* Rider

The complete delivery workflow was tested:

**Retailer creates delivery → Dispatcher assigns rider → Rider picks up delivery → Rider delivers delivery**

## UX Findings

### 1. Was it easy to understand what to do on each screen?

**Yes.**

The screens and workflow were clear enough for the user to understand the required actions for each role.

### 2. Were the buttons and labels clear?

**Yes.**

The main actions and labels were understandable for the Retailer, Dispatcher, and Rider workflows.

### 3. Was it obvious what happened after each action?

**Yes.**

The delivery status changes made it possible to understand the progress of the delivery.

### 4. Was anything confusing or difficult to find?

**Yes.**

Completed deliveries can become difficult to find because they are not separated into a dedicated history section.

## Recommended Improvements

1. Add a **Completed/Delivered History** section so users can easily find deliveries that have already been completed.

2. Separate **active deliveries** from **completed deliveries** so users can immediately distinguish deliveries that still require action from those that are finished.

3. Provide clearer feedback after delivery actions so users can easily understand that an action was successful and what the next step is.

## Conclusion

The core Reflex workflow is understandable and usable across the Retailer, Dispatcher, and Rider roles.

The main usability improvement identified is better visibility of completed deliveries through a dedicated history section.
